# Reflections on *Of Human Bondage*

This article covers:

1. What I would do if I were Philip when meeting each woman in the novel.
2. How this book has influenced my view of life.
3. How it has changed my perspective on love and relationships.
4. Why I see myself as Philip rather than any other character.

---

# If I Were Philip

I think there are a few things I would have done differently.

I won't go over Philip's choices here. I think Philip is about 80% emotional and 20% rational. Since I'm looking at the story from an outside perspective, I'd probably be 70% rational and 30% emotional.

---

## Hedwig

She was the first girl I ever had a crush on.

I wouldn't dare get too close to her or take things any further. She has an outgoing personality and already has someone she likes. It was simply the wrong timing.

Still, I'm grateful to her for giving me those wonderful memories of our daily walks.

---

## Miss Wilkinson

She was much older and more mature.

When I met her, I had just begun to discover love and desperately wanted to experience it.

When she had a fever (the novel never makes it clear whether it was genuine or not), I noticed her rough skin and wrinkles, yet I still loved her.

Later, she cried for no apparent reason and constantly clung to me day and night. Eventually, I became annoyed and started to see her as emotionally immature.

Although she was more than ten years older than me, I probably would have said some hurtful things, hoping she could grow from them.

In the end, I would openly admit that I had wasted some of her precious years, sincerely ask for her forgiveness, and hope we could remain good friends.

---

## Fanny Price

She's extremely talkative, which makes it difficult for me to develop any romantic feelings for her.

Still, I would always feel grateful toward her. Whatever she asked of me, I would do my best to help.

Of course, she never showed any affection for me, so I never truly understood how she felt until after her death.

---

## Ruth Chalice

She has a relaxed, open-minded, and carefree personality.

At first, I didn't realize how wonderful she was.

Once I did, I would probably have the same fantasies Philip had, though I still wouldn't dare expect too much.

She's just far too carefree. Haha.

---

## Mildred

At first, her rude behavior made it impossible for me to like her.

As I gradually learned more about her background, I began to understand her vanity and pride. Like Philip, I would probably try to "teach her a lesson."

Then I'd slowly fall deeper and deeper until I became completely obsessed.

After she left me, I met Norah, and life became joyful again.

If Mildred came back, I would comfort her—but only emotionally.

I wouldn't give her money or provide her with a place to live.

Again, this is based on being 70% rational. Meeting someone as kind, funny, and emotionally healthy as Norah is incredibly rare.

---

## Norah

After Mildred left for the first time, I met Norah.

I would tell her everything honestly.

When Mildred returned, I would comfort Mildred together with Norah.

As for everything that happened to Mildred afterward, I wouldn't want to get involved.

> **Side Note**
>
> Philip's treatment of Norah truly broke my heart.
>
> Other people's feelings mattered to him, but didn't Norah's feelings matter just as much?
>
> He even tried to make her angry so they could have a big fight, hoping it would ease his own guilt.
>
> When Norah needed comfort the most, Philip disappeared.
>
> Even though he created that painful situation himself, he could have at least tried to ease her suffering.
>
> Reading those chapters made me feel frustrated that Philip didn't appreciate someone as wonderful as Norah.

---

## Sally

Personally, my story would have ended with Norah.

But if we really had separated, I would treasure the next woman I met even more.

In the novel, that happened to be Sally, but honestly, I think I would cherish anyone who came into my life after that.

Being yourself is enough.

Loving someone too much can become another kind of prison.

---

# Reflections on Life

I believe Cronshaw had the greatest influence on Philip.

To me, the meaning of life is like a Persian carpet—something each person must discover for themselves.

The greatest prison rarely comes from other people.

More often than not, it is created by the meaning we attach to our own desires.

Life feels like an endless cycle of growth:

> Belief → Experience → Setbacks → Doubt → Transformation → Reconstruction → Practice → Experience → Reflection → Transformation Again → Reconstruction Again

Growth is not a straight line like **y = x**.

It's exponential, like **y = eˣ**.

Of course, life doesn't always move upward.

Sometimes we fall backward.

Sometimes we retreat.

But eventually, we must face our difficulties and overcome them.

---

# Reflections on Love

### Approach a woman without putting her on a pedestal.

---

### Don't confuse desire with destiny. Desire is only the voice of your emotions.

---

### Love reveals your wounds before it reveals your soulmate.

---

### Rejection exposes the story you tell yourself about who you are.

---

### Freedom begins when desire no longer controls your choices.

---

### Appreciate intimacy without treating sex as a measure of your worth.

---

# Why Am I Philip Instead of Someone Else?

Because I believe I will never stop growing.

本篇主要讲述：

# 假设我是菲利普

我可能有一些事情做得不太一样。

菲利普本人的做法这里不再阐述。菲利普可能八分感性，两分理性；我由于是上帝视角，所以我七分理性，三分感性。

---

## 遇到赫德维格

她是我暗恋的第一个人。

我不敢太靠近她，不敢推进一步。她性格开朗，也有心意的对象。只是遇到的时机不合适，感谢她给我日常散步的美好时光的回忆。

---

## 遇到威尔金森小姐

她年龄成熟。

我遇到她的时候，我情窦初开，渴望爱情。她发烧的时候（原文没说是不是借口），我发现她粗糙的皮肤、皱褶，依然爱她。

后来她无缘无故哭，一天 24 小时一直撵着我，让我产生讨厌的心情，感觉她很幼稚。

她虽然大我 10 多岁，我会说些难听的话，希望她能成长。

然后呢，我会大大方方耽误了她的美好年华，恳请她原谅我，成为好朋友。

---

## 遇到范妮・普莱斯

她的嘴真的很碎，让我产生不了任何的好感。

但是我内心会对她有感激之心，她无论要求什么，我尽量满足。

当然，她没表现出任何对我的好感，所以我内心不知道她对我的态度，直到她去世。

---

## 遇到露思・查利斯

她性格松弛、开放洒脱。

一开始没发现她很好，后来发现她很好之后，我会和菲利普本人一样心有幻想，但是也不敢想太多。

毕竟太洒脱哈哈哈哈哈。

---

## 遇到米尔德里德

她粗俗的行为，一开始让我感受不到任何好感。

但是逐渐了解她家境，慢慢明白她爱慕虚荣、好面子，我会和菲利普本人一样“给她点颜色瞧瞧”。

结果越陷越深，逐渐上头，无法自拔。

当她抛弃我之后，我遇到了诺拉，我感受到快乐。

但是当她又回来的时候，我会安慰她，但是仅仅局限于安慰，不会给她钱、给她安排住处什么的。

我再次强调，我这是基于七分理性的情况下，因为遇到性格超级棒、还会逗人发笑的诺拉真得很不容易。

---

## 遇到诺拉

在与米尔德里德第一次分开之后，我遇到了诺拉。

我会和诺拉坦白一切。

后来米尔德里德回来了，我会和诺拉一起安慰米尔德里德。

至于米尔德里德后面的事情，我不想关心。

> **题外话：**
>
> 菲利普本人对诺拉的行为让我很痛心。
>
> 他对别人的感情是感情，她对他的感情不是感情吗？
>
> 还故意想让诺拉生气，大吵一架，这样心理道德上会愧疚少点。
>
> 在诺拉最需要你菲利普安慰的时候，菲利普却不见踪影。
>
> 虽然说这样的窘境是他制造的，但是至少要让她不要那么心痛。
>
> 我看这些内容的时候，埋怨菲利普本人不懂得珍惜，唉。

---

## 遇到莎莉

其实我个人遇到诺拉已经结束了。

但是如果真的和诺拉分开了，我会格外珍惜下一个人。

只是这里遇到了莎莉，其他人我也会同样非常珍惜。

做自己就好，太爱别人会陷入另外一个漩涡。

---

# 生活感悟

我认为克朗肖是对菲利普帮助最大的。

人生的意义就是一块波斯小地毯，人生需要自己探索。

一个人最大的牢笼，很少来自别人；几乎总是来自自己赋予欲望的意义。

人生像是一个不断循环、不断升级的过程：

> 相信 → 体验 → 碰壁 → 怀疑 → 颠覆 → 重建 → 实践 → 经历 → 反思 → 再次颠覆 → 再次重建

人生不是 **y = x** 的逐渐成长，而是 **y = eˣ** 的指数级成长。

当然啦，并不是一直向上，也可以向下。遇到了困难，可以退缩，但是最终还要克服。

---

---

### 拒绝会暴露你对自己的看法。

---

### 当欲望不再支配你的选择时，自由才真正开始。

### 享受亲密关系，但不要把性当成衡量自己价值的标准。

# 为什么我是菲利普，而不是其他男生或者女生

因为我相信自己会一直成长。
